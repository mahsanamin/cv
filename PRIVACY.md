# Privacy Policy

This CV website uses Google Analytics to understand visitor behavior and improve the user experience.

## Information Collected

- Page views and session duration
- Geographic location (country/region level)
- Device type and browser information
- Referral sources (how visitors found this CV)

## Purpose

Analytics help me understand:
- How many people view my professional profile
- Which sections are most engaging
- Geographic reach of my professional network
- Effectiveness of CV distribution channels

## Data Usage

- Information is used solely for professional development purposes
- No personal identification of individual visitors
- Data is not shared with third parties beyond Google Analytics
- Analytics data helps improve CV content and presentation

## Your Rights

- You can opt out of analytics tracking using browser settings
- No cookies store personal information
- All analytics comply with standard web privacy practices
